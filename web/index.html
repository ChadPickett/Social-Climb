    Use it on your phone

    Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as this computer, then scan this code with the phone's camera.

    Once it opens, tap Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen to get an app icon.

    Can't connect? When Windows asks whether to allow Social Climb on the network, click Allow. The app only works on your phone while it's running on this computer.
